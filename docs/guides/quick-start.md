# SN HR Portal Quick Start Guide

A concise reference for getting started with the SN HR Portal.

---

## Login

1. Navigate to the portal login page
2. Enter your company email and password
3. Click "Sign in"

You will be redirected to your role-specific dashboard automatically.

---

## Your Role at a Glance

### Regular Employee

**Your Navigation:**
- Home (Dashboard)
- My 201 Files (Documents)
- Onboarding (Task checklists)
- Performance (OKRs, KPIs, Reviews)
- Payroll (Invoice submission)
- Information Hub (Announcements)
- Profile (Personal info)

**First Steps:**
1. Check your dashboard for pending tasks
2. Upload any missing documents in My 201 Files
3. Complete onboarding checklist items
4. Review your performance objectives

### Manager

**Additional Navigation:**
- Team Performance (Monitor team)

**Key Tasks:**
1. Review team member performance
2. Complete pending manager reviews
3. Monitor OKR/KPI progress across team

### Intern

**Your Navigation:**
- Dashboard (Progress overview)
- My Reports (Daily submissions)
- Profile (Personal info)

**Daily Workflow:**
1. Log your hours daily
2. Submit EOD (End of Day) report before leaving
3. Track your progress toward required hours

### HR Administrator

**Your Navigation:**
- Probation Tracker (Employee monitoring)
- Performance (Organization settings)
- Intern Management (All interns)

**Key Tasks:**
1. Monitor employees on probation
2. Conduct performance appraisals
3. Oversee intern progress

### COS Approver

**Your Navigation:**
- Invoice Approvals

**Key Tasks:**
1. Review pending invoices
2. Approve or reject with notes
3. Track processed invoices

## Common Tasks

### Upload a Document

1. Go to **My 201 Files**
2. Find the document card (shows "Not Uploaded")
3. Click **Upload Document** button
4. Drag and drop your file or click **Choose File**
5. Click **Upload**

### Submit an Invoice

1. Go to **Payroll**
2. Click **Submit Invoice**
3. Select the pay period
4. Enter the amount
5. Upload your invoice document
6. Add optional notes
7. Click **Submit Invoice**

### Complete an Onboarding Task

1. Go to **Onboarding**
2. Expand the relevant category
3. Click **Complete** on the task
4. Follow any additional instructions

### Submit an EOD Report (Interns)

1. Go to **Dashboard**
2. Click **Submit EOD Report**
3. Enter tasks completed
4. Enter hours logged
5. Add learnings and challenges (optional)
6. Click **Submit**

### Review Team Performance (Managers)

1. Go to **Team Performance**
2. Find team member with "Pending My Review" status
3. Click **Review** button
4. Rate OKRs and KPIs
5. Provide overall rating and feedback
6. Click **Submit Appraisal**

### Approve/Reject an Invoice (COS)

1. Go to **Invoice Approvals**
2. Use the carousel or table to find pending invoice
3. Click **View Document** to review
4. Click **Approve** or **Reject**
5. Add notes (required for rejection)
6. Confirm action

## Getting Help

**AI Assistant**
- Click the chat bubble in the bottom-left corner
- Ask questions about HR policies, documents, or processes

**Need More Help?**
- Contact IT Support for technical issues
- Contact HR for policy questions

## Tips

- Check your dashboard daily for pending actions
- Keep documents in PDF, JPG, or PNG format (max 10MB)
- Submit invoices before the deadline
- Interns: Submit EOD reports every working day
- Use the Information Hub to stay updated on announcements
