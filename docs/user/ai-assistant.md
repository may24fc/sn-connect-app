# AI HR Assistant

The AI-powered HR Assistant is available to all users. It can answer questions about company policies, HR processes, documents, and more.

## Opening the Assistant

Click the **floating chat button** (message icon) in the **bottom-left corner** of any page. The chat panel slides open.

## Using the Chat

1. Type your question in the text input at the bottom of the panel
2. Press **Enter** or click the **Send** button
3. Wait for the AI to respond — a loading indicator shows while it's processing
4. The response appears as a message bubble with a timestamp

You can ask follow-up questions in the same conversation. The AI remembers the context of your current chat session.

## What You Can Ask

| Topic | Example Questions |
|-------|-------------------|
| **Documents** | "How do I upload my NBI clearance?" |
| **Payroll** | "When is my invoice due?" / "How do I submit an invoice?" |
| **Leave** | "How do I request leave?" / "What's the vacation policy?" |
| **Onboarding** | "What documents do I need to submit?" |
| **Benefits** | "What benefits am I eligible for?" |
| **Performance** | "How does the OKR process work?" |
| **Policies** | "What is the company dress code?" |
| **Tasks** | "How do I update my task status?" |

The AI draws from the company's knowledge base, which includes policies, handbooks, FAQs, procedures, and guidelines uploaded by HR and administrators.

## How It Works

The assistant uses **Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG)**:

1. Your question is matched against the HR knowledge base
2. Relevant policy documents and procedures are retrieved
3. The AI generates an answer based on those specific sources
4. Responses stream in real-time as they're generated

## Tips for Better Answers

- **Be specific** — "How many sick leave days do I get per year?" works better than "Tell me about leave"
- **One topic at a time** — Ask focused questions for more accurate answers
- **Ask follow-ups** — If the first answer isn't enough, ask for clarification

## Chat Controls

| Control | Action |
|---------|--------|
| **Minimize** | Collapse the chat panel |
| **Maximize** | Expand to full panel |
| **Close** | Close the chat entirely |

## Important Notes

- The AI provides guidance based on uploaded company policies. Always verify with HR for official decisions.
- A disclaimer at the bottom of the chat reminds you that AI responses may not always be perfectly accurate.
- The knowledge base is maintained by HR administrators and may not cover every topic.
