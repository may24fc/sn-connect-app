# Ultimate Health Project Task Tracker

## Current Status

UHP Outreach Tracker reply-derived cards now update optimistically with exact rollback and server reconciliation. Metrics-period changes remain server-confirmed, but they fetch independently and immediately instead of waiting for the client-table search debounce. The legacy-reply backfill is applied locally and in production. All three UHP n8n workflows now have a verified Telegram test destination and successful Control Hub delivery records. Portal Reminders is active; the Volume Points digest and Outreach summary remain inactive pending the recipient and activation decision.

The Outreach cards now include Last week and attribute later replies to the week a client was contacted, unless a newer follow-up owns the reply. The Wellness evaluations card counts a WE form Filled status milestone or a wellness appointment once per client in the selected activity period. The table's Updated date is not used for the card period.

The Outreach table now has an explicit Call Yes/No toggle. Enabling it timestamps the call for the selected-period Calls card and Friday digest, while deduplicating a client who also has a logged call activity. The migration is applied and verified locally and in production.

The Metrics period control also supports an inclusive custom start/end date range. Draft dates remain local until Apply date range is selected, then the cards refresh through the existing server-confirmed request path. The field and Apply/Clear range control are shared with Marketing Reports instead of maintaining separate calendars.

Related reliability review: [optimization.md](optimization.md).
The shared period rollout and its measurements are in [Metrics Period Consistency](../metrics-period-consistency/task-tracker.md).
