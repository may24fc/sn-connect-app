# SN AI Quest — Proposal and Open Questions

*Draft for HR review · 16 November 2026*

---

## Summary

**Goal:** help everyone at SN learn AI, apply it to real work, and buy back time for higher-value work. This should be a shared learning programme, not just a competition.

**What we recommend**

- Run **SN AI Quest** as a monthly programme with a **retro-game theme** and original SN artwork.
- Start with **3 groups**, each guided by one AI & Dev team member. As people gain confidence, move to **pairs**, and then to **individuals** competing for the monthly **AI Champion** award.
- Hold a **10-minute weekly AI coaching session** during an existing stand-up.
- Track progress, roles, recordings, and case studies in **SN Control Hub**. Build it in phases after a first pilot month that runs without new software.
- Measure impact through **verified time saved, quality, adoption, and learning**, not just self-reported hours.

**Points we need to align on**

| Topic | Options on the table |
|---|---|
| Who competes | Individuals, pairs, or groups |
| The 5-hour rule | Required from the start, or only once people have built confidence |
| When the work happens | Fully outside work hours, or short coaching during work hours |
| The A$100 prize | Works well for one person, but very little if split across a group |

**Decisions needed first**

1. **Voluntary or required, and on whose time.** This has employment-law implications (see §7.1).
2. **How groups compete and how the prize works** in the group stage.
3. **Launch month, headcount, and how much time the AI & Dev team can give.**

## 1. How It Works: The Monthly Quest

Each cycle follows four stages:

| Week | Stage | What happens |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | **AI Discovery** | Kickoff: AI tools, practical uses, and how AI can help our work |
| 2 | **AI Exploration** | Each group picks a repetitive or time-consuming task and explores AI solutions |
| 3 | **AI Experiment** | Test the idea and record time and process before and after AI. Small improvements count. |
| 4 | **AI Showcase** | Share results, teach others how it was done, and celebrate |

**Cycle length:** one week is short for beginners to run an experiment. We recommend a **2-month cycle for the first stage**, then monthly cycles once people are comfortable.

**Which meeting hosts what:** the monthly core team call has a 5-minute AI segment. That isn't enough time for three group showcases. We suggest:
- **Monthly call (5 min):** announce the winner and show one highlight.
- **Full Showcase:** an optional 30-minute session, or a stand-up slot, where every group presents.

## 2. Groupings

### Groups or pairs?

**Recommendation: progress in stages ("Worlds").**

| Stage | Unit | Timing | Why |
|---|---|---|---|
| **World 1** | 3 groups | Months 1–2 (one cycle) | Low pressure. Beginners learn alongside others, and coaching is manageable. |
| **World 2** | Smaller groups or pairs | Months 3–4 | Everyone contributes more visibly, and more ideas run at the same time. |
| **World 3** | Individuals | Month 5 onward | The full AI Champion award: one person, at least 5 hours saved, with a case study. |

A group moves to the next World when it meets a clear bar. For example: *the group shipped at least one working AI use case that most of its members now use.*

### Basis for grouping

**Recommendation: group people who do similar work** (same function or department cluster), with **one AI & Dev team member in each group as its AI Guide**.

- When a group does similar work, one solution can help most of its members.
- The AI Guide coaches and unblocks. **They don't build the solution for the group**, or the group won't learn anything.
- Balance groups for size, AI confidence (mix beginners with early adopters), and schedules or time zones.
- We need **at least 3 AI & Dev members** available as Guides. If fewer are available, we need fewer groups.

## 3. AI Encouragement and Coaching

### Kickoff session

**Recommendation: HR (May) leads the kickoff, and the CEO opens it** with 2–3 minutes on why this matters. The AI & Dev team gives a 10-minute live demo in the same session so people see real examples, not just talk.

### Coaching in stand-ups

**Recommendation: yes, as one short slot a week. Don't take over whole stand-ups.**

- **Power-Up Session:** 10 minutes once a week, run by the AI & Dev team. Each session covers one tool, one live demo, and one real SN use case.
- Once groups start shipping results, they present their own Power-Up Sessions. People learn from colleagues, not just the experts.
- Sessions are recorded and saved to the **Power-Up Library** (§6) for anyone who missed them.

**Note:** the original brief says learning and building should happen outside work hours. Our suggested compromise is that the **10-minute coaching happens during work hours** as company training, and **experimenting and building happen on personal time**. This needs management approval and an employment check (§7.1).

## 4. SN Control Hub Integration and Gamification

### Theme

**Recommendation: a retro platformer style with original artwork.** Generic game elements like coins, pipes, worlds, castles, and levels are fine. We shouldn't use characters, logos, music, or sound effects from commercial games such as Super Mario, because they're trademarked and copyrighted. The current SN AI Quest artwork, with original SN characters, already does this well.

### What Control Hub will provide

Control Hub already has two similar features we can build on: **Wellness Bingo**, a buddy challenge with monthly cycles and weekly recording links, and **staff recognition**. The Quest can follow the same approach. Bingo only supports pairs, though, so groups need some new setup.

| Phase | What's included | Rough timing* |
|---|---|---|
| **Phase 0 – Pilot** | Run the first cycle with simple tools: groups and roles in a shared sheet, recording links, and case studies through a simple form. No new software needed. | Can start right away |
| **Phase 1 – Quest basics** | Quest cycles and the four stages, groups with member roles, weekly progress logs, weekly recording links, case-study submission, and an admin review screen | About 3 weeks |
| **Phase 2 – Game layer** | Coins, levels, badges, the quest board, the group leaderboard, and the retro visuals | About 2 weeks |
| **Phase 3 – Library and insights** | Power-Up Library, impact dashboard (hours saved, cost vs. benefit), and support for pairs and individuals | About 2 weeks |

\*Timings are rough. They depend on final scope and AI & Dev availability. The same team also guides groups and runs coaching (see §7.6).

**Suggested launch:** run Phase 0 in the first month while Phase 1 is built.

### Recorded videos

**Recommendation: yes, but keep it light.**
- **One recording of 10 minutes or less per group per week**, such as a walkthrough of the experiment. Recording every meeting is too much to produce and too much for anyone to review.
- Share recordings as **links** (Loom, Google Drive, or Meet).
- Get every member's **consent** before recording. Recordings must **never show client data, payroll, medical, bank, or login details**.

### Member roles and accountability

Each member has a role and logs a short weekly entry in Control Hub: their role, what they did, and time spent.

| Role | Responsibility |
|---|---|
| **Quest Captain** | Runs group meetings, keeps the timeline, submits on time |
| **AI Guide** (AI & Dev member) | Coaching, tool suggestions, checking the idea is practical and safe |
| **Scout** | Finds the task to improve and records the **time it takes before AI** |
| **Builders** (everyone else) | Try the AI solution in their own work and record the time after AI |
| **Scribe** | Writes the case study and owns the weekly recording |
| **Presenter** | Presents at the Showcase |

- **Rotate roles each cycle** so everyone tries different responsibilities.
- **Small groups (4 or fewer):** combine roles, e.g. Captain + Presenter and Scout + Scribe.

### Visibility and privacy

- **World 1 shows group rankings only.** Ranking individuals publicly before people have built confidence can put them off.
- **Progress logs** are visible to the group, its AI Guide, HR, and admins.
- **Recordings** are visible to the group, HR, and admins. Keep them for **90 days after the cycle ends**, unless the group agrees to add them to the Power-Up Library.

## 5. Scoring, Prize, and Measuring Impact

### Defining "5 hours saved"

**Proposed definition:** at least **5 hours saved every month on an ongoing basis**, verified, and still in use **30 days later**.
- **World 1 and 2:** 5 hours per month as a **group total**.
- **World 3:** 5 hours per month **per person**, matching the original AI Champion award.
- Smaller savings still earn coins and recognition. The 5-hour bar applies only to the cash prize.

### Scoring rubric (suggested)

| Area | Weight | What's assessed |
|---|---|---|
| Time saved | 40% | Verified ongoing hours saved per month |
| Adoption and reuse | 25% | How many people use the solution, and whether other teams adopted it |
| Documentation | 20% | Quality of the case study and recordings, and how easy it is for others to copy |
| Learning and helping | 15% | Stages completed, Power-Up Sessions given, help given to other groups |

- **Judges:** the CEO, HR, and one AI & Dev member. A Guide doesn't score their own group.
- **Tie-break:** the entry with higher adoption and reuse wins.
- **Fairness:** some roles have more repetitive work than others. Score time saved both in hours and as a **percentage of the original task time**, so roles with less repetitive work can still compete.

### Prize

- **A$100 per month** for the overall winner, as in the original brief.
- **For groups, choose one:** (a) A$100 for a team lunch or shared reward, (b) a larger group prize during World 1 and 2, or (c) A$100 to the group plus non-cash badges for each member.
- **Free extra recognition:** badges for *Most Reused*, *Best Beginner Win*, and *Best Documentation*.
- **Payment:** confirm with Finance whether the prize is paid as a payroll bonus or a gift card, and how it's taxed.

### Measuring impact

| What | How it's measured | Who captures it |
|---|---|---|
| **Time saved** | (Time before AI − time with AI) × how often the task happens = hours saved per month | The Scout records "before". Builders record "after". A verifier signs off. |
| **Cost vs. benefit** | Hours saved × hourly cost, compared with AI tool costs | Quest submissions plus AI spending records |
| **Quality** | Errors and rework before vs. after, and a 1–5 quality rating | Signed off by whoever receives the output |
| **Adoption** | % of staff taking part, solutions shipped, solutions still used after 30 and 60 days, reuse by other teams | Control Hub Quest data |
| **Learning** | Self-rated AI confidence (1–5) at the start and end of each cycle | A two-question pulse survey |

**To keep results credible:**
- Record the "before" time **before** the AI change.
- The verifier is **not a member of the submitting group**.
- Check at **30 days** that the solution is still in use.

## 6. Keeping It Engaging, Practical, and Sustainable

1. **Power-Up Library:** every approved solution is saved as a reusable guide (the task, the tool, the prompt, the steps, and the time saved), so learning builds up instead of disappearing after the Showcase.
2. **More than a competition:** coins reward learning and sharing, not just results. Failed experiments that are well documented also earn coins, because knowing what doesn't work is useful too.
3. **The Showcase is for teaching:** each group explains *how* they did it, so others can copy it.
4. **Start simple:** World 1 uses only **stages, coins, and one badge**. Add reuse bonuses, helper coins, extra badges, and a quarterly "Boss Level" challenge once the basics are working.
5. **Keep the load light:** one call segment, one 10-minute coaching session a week, and one short recording a week. If it feels like homework, people will drop out.

## 7. Policies and Risks

### 7.1 Voluntary or required, and on whose time

If roles, logs, and recordings are **required** but done **outside work hours**, that time may legally count as working time under Philippine and Australian employment rules. **HR should confirm with legal or an employment adviser before launch.** Options:
- **Voluntary participation** outside work hours, or
- **Required participation**, with a set amount of time allowed during work hours.

### 7.2 What happens to the time saved

People may read "save 5 hours" as "more work" or "fewer jobs". We recommend the CEO says clearly that time saved goes to **higher-value work and personal development**, and that the programme isn't linked to job cuts. People need to feel safe taking part for this to work.

### 7.3 AI tool access and cost

- Agree a list of **approved AI tools**.
- Free versions of some tools may use what people type in to train their models. Where staff handle company data, **company-paid business plans** are safer.
- Set a **tool budget** alongside the prize budget.

### 7.4 AI Safe-Use Rules (required)

- Never put **client personal data, payroll, bank, medical, or login details** into AI tools.
- Use **approved tools only**.
- A **person reviews** all AI output before it goes to a client.
- Every Quest submission confirms these rules were followed.

### 7.5 Who owns and maintains what is built

- Every solution has a **named owner** in the group who is responsible for keeping it working.
- Any solution that touches **client systems, live company data, or shared logins** needs AI & Dev approval before it's used.
- The AI & Dev team isn't the default maintainer of every group's tool.

### 7.6 AI & Dev team role and capacity

- **AI & Dev members act as Guides and judges, and aren't eligible for the prize.** They earn recognition for coaching instead.
- The same team guides groups (about 1–2 hours per group per week), runs weekly coaching, and builds Control Hub. If capacity is tight, **extend the pilot (Phase 0) or delay Phases 2–3**.

### 7.7 Special cases

| Situation | Suggested approach |
|---|---|
| New hire mid-cycle | Joins at the next cycle, or joins a group as a Builder |
| On leave during a cycle | Their role is covered by rotation, with no penalty |
| Part-time staff | Expectations adjusted to their hours |
| Roles where AI fits less naturally | Learning, documentation, and helping still count toward the score |

## 8. Pilot Review

At the **end of World 1 (Month 2)**, decide whether to continue, adjust, or stop. Suggested success measures:

- **70% or more** of staff take part
- **At least 3** working AI solutions shipped
- **Verified hours saved** reported for each group
- A **positive** pulse survey result, with AI confidence going up
