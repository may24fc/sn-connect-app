# SN AI Quest — Proposal v2

*Updated after HR feedback · 16 November 2026 · Launch: December 2026*

---

## Summary

**SN AI Quest** is a **voluntary** team programme to help everyone at SN learn AI, try it on real work, and gradually buy back time for higher-value work. The focus is on **learning and steady improvement**, not just competition.

**Agreed**

- **Launch in December 2026**, with a kickoff led and officially opened by **May**.
- **Participation is voluntary.** Groups are formed once we know how many people have joined.
- **Progression:** individual exploration → **3 groups** → **pairs** → **individuals**.
- **One AI & Dev team member guides each group** and is eligible for the prize.
- **A 10-minute AI coaching session every week**, during working hours, as part of the stand-up.
- **Everyone can see everyone's progress** in SN Control Hub. Only the judges can watch the recordings.
- **Judges score submissions directly in Control Hub.**
- **Any amount of time saved counts.** There is no 5-hour minimum.
- **A retro-game theme with original SN characters**, inspired by classic platform games.

**Awaiting approval or discussion**

- The **prize**: A$250 for the winning group (§8)
- How the **pilot will be evaluated** (§13)

## 2. Progression System

### Levels: progress within a cycle

Each participant or group moves through five levels. Their current level shows on the Control Hub progress tracker.

| Level | Name | What it means | Done when… | World 1 timing |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **1** | **Exploration** | Learn and try AI tools | Attended coaching and tried at least 2 tools on real work | December (individual) |
| **2** | **Identifying an Opportunity** | Pick a repetitive or slow task to improve | Task chosen and **"before AI" time recorded** | 4–15 Jan |
| **3** | **Developing an Automation Project** | Build the AI solution | A working first version, shown in a demo recording | 18 Jan – 5 Feb |
| **4** | **Testing** | Use it in real work and fix issues | Used on real work at least 5 times, with **"after AI" time recorded** | 8–19 Feb |
| **5** | **Execution / Implementation** | Roll it out for everyday use | Members use it in their daily work, the final submission is sent, and the group presents at the Showcase | 22–26 Feb |

In the shorter 4-week cycles of World 2 and World 3, Levels 2–5 take about one week each.

**Moving up a level:** the group submits a short update and a demo recording in Control Hub. HR confirms the level, and the progress tracker updates for everyone to see.

### Worlds: progress across cycles

| World | Who works together | When | Moves on when… |
|---|---|---|---|
| **World 1** | 3 groups of 4–5 members | Jan–Feb 2027 | The pilot review approves, and each group reached Level 5 |
| **World 2** | Pairs | Mid-Mar – Apr 2027 | Pairs reach Level 5 in at least one cycle |
| **World 3** | Individuals | May 2027 onward | Ongoing monthly AI Champion |

## 3. Groups

- **Sign-up:** voluntary, 1–11 December.
- **Group size:** 4–5 members plus one AI Guide from the AI & Dev team.
- **Basis:** where possible, group people who do **similar work**, so one solution can help most members. Mix confident and new AI users.
- **If sign-up numbers differ from 12–15:**
  - Fewer than 9 volunteers: 2 groups.
  - Fewer than 6: start directly with pairs.
  - More than 15: groups of 6, or add a fourth Guide if AI & Dev capacity allows.
- **Late joiners** join at the start of the next cycle.

**Member roles** (rotate each cycle; combine roles in groups of 4):

| Role | Responsibility |
|---|---|
| **Quest Captain** | Keeps the group on schedule and submits level updates |
| **Scout** | Finds the task to improve and records the "before AI" time |
| **Builders** | Build and test the solution in their own work, and record the "after AI" time |
| **Scribe** | Writes the case study and records the demo videos |
| **Presenter** | Presents at the Showcase |
| **AI Guide** (AI & Dev) | Coaches, suggests tools, and checks the solution is safe. **Guides the group, but doesn't build the solution for them.** |

## 5. Weekly AI Coaching (Power-Up Session)

- **10 minutes, once a week, during working hours**, as part of the regular stand-up.
- **The AI & Dev team facilitates** and takes over the stand-up for that slot.
- **Format:** one tool, one live demo, one real SN example.
- **Starts the week of 7 December** and runs throughout the programme.
- From World 1 onward, groups also take turns presenting what they've built.
- Sessions are saved to the **Power-Up Library** in Control Hub for anyone who missed them.

Quest work beyond the weekly coaching is voluntary and done in participants' own time, as the Founder intended.

## 6. SN Control Hub: Progress, Visibility, and Scoring

### Theme

The theme is a retro platform-game style, with **original SN characters and artwork**. Classic platform games are the inspiration only: we won't use any characters, logos, music, or sounds from existing games.

### What everyone can see

- The **progress tracker**: each group's current level (1–5) and each member's role
- **Coins and badges** earned
- **Groups' total hours saved** (§9)
- The **Power-Up Library** of approved solutions and coaching sessions

### What only judges can see

- **Recordings** (progress updates and demos)
- **Judges' scores**, until the winner is announced

### Judge scoring in Control Hub

- Judges open each final submission in Control Hub, review the case study and recordings, and **score each area from 1 to 5** (§8).
- Control Hub calculates the weighted total and the ranking.
- Judges can leave comments, which are shared with the group after the winner is announced.

### Coins and badges (start simple)

- **Coins** for each level completed.
- **Badges:** *5-Hour Hero* (5 or more hours saved per month), *Most Used*, *Best Demo*, *Helping Hand* (for helping other groups).
- More game features can be added after the pilot review, based on feedback.

## 7. Recordings

- Recordings **show progress and demonstrate the work**. They are **not recordings of meetings**.
- **One short recording (5–10 minutes) per level**, submitted with each level update:
  - **Level 2:** the task done the current way (shows the "before")
  - **Level 3:** a demo of the first working version
  - **Level 4:** the solution being used on real work
  - **Level 5:** the final demo
- Recordings are shared as links (Loom, Google Drive) and **only judges can view them**.
- Recordings must **never show client personal data, payroll, bank, medical, or login details**. Use test or blurred data.

## 9. Time Saved: How It's Calculated, Documented, and Communicated

### How it's calculated

> **Hours saved per month = (minutes before AI − minutes with AI) × times done per month ÷ 60**

**Example 1:** a weekly report takes 60 minutes and is done 4 times a month. With AI it takes 20 minutes.
(60 − 20) × 4 ÷ 60 = **2.7 hours saved per month**. This counts, because any saving counts.

**Example 2:** client follow-up emails take 15 minutes each, 40 times a month. With AI they take 5 minutes.
(15 − 5) × 40 ÷ 60 = **6.7 hours saved per month**. This earns the *5-Hour Hero* badge.

### How it's documented

| Step | When | What's recorded in Control Hub | Who |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Before AI** | Level 2 | The task, how often it's done, and minutes per time, timed **before** any change, plus a short recording of the current process | Scout |
| **With AI** | Level 4 | Average minutes per time across at least 5 real uses | Builders |
| **Verified** | Level 5 | Judges check the before and after figures against the recordings | Judges |
| **Still in use** | 30 days after the Showcase | A quick check that the solution is still being used | Solution owner (§10) |

### How it's communicated

- **Control Hub:** total hours saved per group and for the whole programme, visible to everyone.
- **Monthly core team call:** the programme total and one success story.
- **Showcase:** each group presents its before and after figures.
- **Founder's message at kickoff:** time saved through AI goes to **higher-value work and personal growth**. It isn't linked to job cuts or extra workload. This helps people take part with confidence.

## 10. Solution Ownership

A **solution** is anything a group builds: a saved prompt, a template, an AI workflow, or an automation.

**Why it matters:** if nobody owns a solution, it stops working when a tool changes or a person moves on, and the time savings disappear.

### How it works

1. **At Level 3, the group names one owner** for each solution, usually the Builder who uses it most.
2. **The owner is responsible for:**
   - Writing a **one-page how-to guide** (what it does, the steps, and the tools and prompts used) for the Power-Up Library
   - Making sure it **still works**, and doing the 30-day check (§9)
   - Updating the guide if the tool or process changes
   - **Handing over** to another group member if they change roles or leave
3. **The AI Guide reviews safety before Level 5:** anything that touches client data, live company systems, or shared logins needs AI & Dev approval before everyday use.
4. **After the cycle**, the solution stays with the owner's team. The AI & Dev team helps if something breaks, but **doesn't take over maintaining it**.

### What's expected

| Who | Expectation |
|---|---|
| **Each participant** | Uses the solution in real work and reports problems to the owner |
| **Solution owner** | Maintains the how-to guide, keeps it working, does the 30-day check, hands over if needed |
| **Group** | Names an owner at Level 3 and makes sure the how-to guide is in the Library before the Showcase |
| **AI Guide** | Safety review before Level 5, and occasional help afterward |

## 11. AI Tools and Safe Use

- Use only tools on the **approved AI tools list**, to be agreed before kickoff.
- **Never** put client personal data, payroll, bank, medical, or login details into AI tools.
- A **person checks** all AI output before it goes to a client.
- Each level submission confirms these rules were followed.

## 13. Pilot Review (Draft for Discussion)

**Proposed review date:** week of 8 March 2027, after the World 1 Showcase.

**Proposed measures.** Because participation is voluntary, these measure engagement among the people who joined:

| Measure | Proposed target |
|---|---|
| Sign-ups | At least 12 volunteers (enough for 3 groups) |
| Completion | At least 2 of 3 groups reach Level 5 |
| Solutions in real use | At least 3 solutions still used 30 days later |
| Time saved | Any verified saving from each group. The total is reported, with no minimum. |
| Learning | Average self-rated AI confidence goes up between December and February (two-question survey) |
| Feedback | Most participants would join again |

**Possible outcomes:**
- **Continue** to World 2 as planned.
- **Adjust:** change the format, timeline, or prize.
- **Pause.**
