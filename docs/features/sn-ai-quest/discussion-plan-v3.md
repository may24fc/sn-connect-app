# SN AI Quest — Proposal v3 (Simplified)

*Updated 18 November 2026 · Launch: December 2026*

---

## The Idea

Each month, **three teams** each build **one AI automation** that helps everyone on their team, and **show proof of the work time it saves**. The team with the best result wins that month's prize.

**There are no points, levels, or coins.** A team only wins with a **working automation that saves real time**, finished by the end of the month.

---

## How It Works

**Teams**
- **3 teams**, formed from the people who volunteer. Participation is voluntary.
- Each team has **one AI member and one Dev member** to guide and support it.

**The automation**
- Each team chooses **any automation** that is **useful to all of its members**.
- It must be **small enough to finish within the month**, and it must be **working and in use** by the deadline.

**Coaching**
- A **10-minute AI coaching session every week** during the stand-up, run by the AI & Dev team.

---

## Monthly Schedule

| Week | What teams do |
|---|---|
| **Week 1** | Choose the task to automate and **time how long it takes now** |
| **Weeks 2–3** | Build the automation and start using it in real work |
| **Week 4** | **Submit proof of time saved** by the last Friday of the month |
| **Monthly core team call** | Each team gives a short showcase, and the **winner is announced** |

Anything not working by the deadline can't win that month.

---

## Proof of Time Saved

Each team submits:

1. **What they automated**, and who on the team uses it.
2. **Time before and after:**

   > **Hours saved per month = (minutes before − minutes after) × times done per month ÷ 60**

   *Example:* a report takes 60 minutes and is done 4 times a month. With the automation it takes 15 minutes.
   (60 − 15) × 4 ÷ 60 = **3 hours saved per month**.

3. **A short demo video (5 minutes or less)** of the automation working. **Only the judges can see it.**

Any amount of time saved counts. There is no minimum.

---

## Judging and Prize

- **Judges:** Founder, COO, and HR.
- **Judged on:**
  1. **Time saved:** verified hours saved per month
  2. **Usefulness:** how many team members actually use it
- **Prize:** **A$250 for the winning team each month**, paid through payroll. *(Subject to approval.)*

---

## Simple Rules

- **Safe use:** never put client personal data, payroll, bank, medical, or login details into AI tools. Use approved tools only.
- **Ownership:** each automation has **one named owner** on the team. The owner keeps it working and writes a short how-to guide so others can use it.
- **Visibility:** each team's automation and hours saved are shared with everyone at the monthly call, and in SN Control Hub.
