# Getting Started

This guide walks you through your first login and the onboarding process.

## Signing In

1. Open the portal URL in your browser (Chrome recommended)
2. Enter your **company email** and **password**
3. Optionally check **"Remember me for 30 days"**
4. Click **Sign In**

You will be redirected to your role's dashboard:

| Role | Landing Page |
|------|-------------|
| Employee | `/dashboard` |
| Associate | `/associate/dashboard` |

### Forgot Password

1. Click **"Forgot password?"** on the login page
2. Enter your email address
3. Check your inbox for a reset link
4. Follow the link to set a new password

## First-Time Onboarding

New employees are redirected to the **Onboarding Wizard** before they can access the rest of the portal. The wizard has four steps:

### Step 1 — Personal Information

Fill in your personal details:

- First name, last name
- Phone number (with country code picker)
- Date of birth
- Gender
- Address

### Step 2 — Payment Information

Enter your bank and payroll details:

- Bank name (dropdown)
- Bank account number
- SSS, PhilHealth, Pag-IBIG, TIN numbers

### Step 3 — Document Upload

Upload required employment documents:

- Valid Government ID
- Profile Photo
- CV / Resume
- Birth Certificate

**File requirements:** PDF, JPG, or PNG — maximum 10 MB per file.

### Step 4 — Review & Submit

Review all the information you entered across the previous steps. If anything is incorrect, click **Back** to fix it. When ready, click **Submit**.

After submission you'll see an **"Onboarding Complete"** confirmation page and gain full access to the portal.

> **Tip:** You can navigate between steps freely. Your progress is saved automatically, so you can return later if needed.

## Associate Setup

Associates go through a separate setup flow at `/associate/setup` before accessing the portal. This collects:

- School and program information
- Start/end dates
- Required hours
- Supervisor assignment

## Signing Out

1. Click your **avatar** or **name** in the top-right header
2. Select **Logout** from the dropdown menu
