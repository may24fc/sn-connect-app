# Tickets

The Tickets page lets you submit HR or IT support requests and track ticket progress.

## Route

- `/tickets`

## What You Can Do

1. Create a ticket with title, description, team, category, and priority.
2. Add optional context such as expected behavior and reproduction steps.
3. Upload supporting attachments (images, PDF, and common document formats).
4. Track status updates in My Submitted Tickets.

If you are an assigned IT handler, you also see an Assigned to Me tab.

## Ticket Teams and Categories

- HR team: policy, people, and process concerns.
- IT team: technical issues, bugs, and tooling support.

Choose the team first, then select the closest category to improve triage speed.

## Status Tracking

Common statuses include:

- Open or in progress
- Resolved
- Closed

Use the page stats to quickly monitor submitted, open, assigned, and resolved counts.

## Attachment Limits

- Up to 5 files per ticket submission
- Typical formats: image, PDF, DOC/DOCX, TXT
