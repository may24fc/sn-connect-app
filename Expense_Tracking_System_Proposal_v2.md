## **Project Proposal: Control Hub Expense Tracking \& Matching System**

*Internal Workflow Documentation (Updated) • July 2026*

## **1. What is this project for?**

This system is **not** for staff to get paid back for things they bought. Instead, it is an internal tracking page to see exactly where company money is being spent on software top-ups and everyday team bills.

It helps the team keep track of requests by matching what the AI staff asked for against what the admins or marketing team actually paid.

## **2. Main Features \& How It Works**

## **A. Logging and Matching Expenses (The Daily Workflow)**

Tracking is broken down by who handles the money and who requests it:

* **AI Interns, Other Interns \& Staffs (Logging Requests):** When AI interns, interns, or regular staff members need more software credits or a top-up, they type it into the tracker manually. This is how they monitor what they are asking for and stay accountable.
* **Admins \& Marketing (Paying \& Uploading Receipts):** The admins and the Marketing team can make direct payments and upload their official receipts into the system.
* **The Matching Queue:** The system automatically checks the AI staff's request logs against the admin or marketing payment logs to make sure the numbers match up perfectly. If anything doesn't match, the system flags it so it can be fixed.

## **B. Month-to-Month Spending Charts**

* **Month-over-Month Comparison:** The dashboard will feature simple charts comparing this month’s total spending directly against last month’s spending. This gives management a clear view of whether costs are going up or down.
